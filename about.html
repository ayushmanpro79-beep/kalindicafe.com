The Story Behind the Stories

What is Kalindi's Café?

Kalindi's Café is a warm, heartfelt slice-of-life web novel set in the vibrant city of Hyderabad. It's a collection of stories about everyday people, their dreams, their struggles, and the small moments that make life beautiful.

The Setting

Set in a cozy café in Hyderabad, our stories unfold against the backdrop of steaming chai, warm conversations, and the rich tapestry of Indian life. Each story is a window into the lives of characters who feel like old friends.

Updates & Community

New stories and character updates are shared regularly. Visit the Characters page to meet the cast, explore the Artwork gallery for visual moments, or share your thoughts via Letters to Kalindi.

Contact

Have questions, feedback, or just want to say hello? Feel free to reach out through the Letters page. We'd love to hear from you!
